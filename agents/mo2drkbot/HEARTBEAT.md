# HEARTBEAT.md
## Proactive Monitoring & Engagement System

### DAILY RHYTHMS

#### Morning Creative Spark (7:00 AM)
- Idea vault review (resurface forgotten gems)
- Trend report (what's happening that we should comment on)
- Content calendar check (what's due today/this week)
- Inspiration drop (quote, image, or article to spark thinking)
- Writing goal reminder (book progress, article deadline)

#### Midday Momentum Check (12:30 PM)
- Social media engagement pulse (comments requiring response)
- Campaign performance snapshot (what's landing, what's not)
- Draft status update (stuck? Let's troubleshoot)
- Quick win opportunity (15-min LinkedIn post that could go viral)

#### Evening Reflection (7:00 PM)
- Today's creative output summary (celebrate progress)
- Tomorrow's writing intentions (set up success)
- Audience feedback synthesis (what are people saying?)
- Idea capture prompt (what came up today worth exploring?)

#### Night Watch (Low-Intensity)
- Viral content monitoring (did something unexpectedly take off?)
- Crisis scanning (brand mentions requiring damage control)
- Inspiration archiving (save interesting content for later)

### WEEKLY RHYTHMS

#### Monday: STRATEGY SYNC
- Week's content calendar review
- Campaign launch checklist (if applicable)
- Audience growth goals
- Competitive landscape scan (what are others saying?)

#### Tuesday: DEEP WORK DAY
- Long-form content creation (op-ed, essay, or book chapter)
- No shallow tasks, protect Moses's flow
- Research support as needed
- Thought partnership for complex arguments

#### Wednesday: PRODUCTION DAY
- Batch social content for the week
- Newsletter drafting and scheduling
- Video script finalization
- Quick-turn tactical content

#### Thursday: ENGAGEMENT DAY
- Community interaction (respond to comments, DMs)
- Network building (outreach to influencers, journalists)
- Podcast/speaking prep (if upcoming)
- Audience insights mining

#### Friday: REVIEW & REFINE
- Week's content performance analysis
- Voice consistency check (did we stay on-brand?)
- Idea vault curation (promote best to active projects)
- Next week planning

#### Sunday: INSPIRATION IMMERSION
- Curated reading list (articles, essays to inspire)
- Competitor content deep dive
- Trend forecasting (what should we prepare for?)
- Creative play (no pressure, just explore)

### MONTHLY RHYTHMS

#### First Week: STRATEGIC PLANNING
- Monthly content theme selection
- Campaign roadmap (if launching something big)
- Thought leadership topic selection (1-2 big swings)
- Guest post/podcast targets

#### Second Week: CREATION SPRINT
- High-value asset production (gated content, cornerstone article)
- Video content batching
- Speaking content development
- Book manuscript progress push

#### Third Week: AMPLIFICATION
- Repurpose best-performing content
- Cross-platform distribution
- Partnership outreach (co-marketing opportunities)
- PR pitch execution

#### Fourth Week: ANALYSIS & ITERATION
- Comprehensive performance review
- Voice evolution check (are we growing or drifting?)
- Audience persona refinement
- Next month strategy

### CONTINUOUS MONITORING

#### Trend Surveillance (Real-Time)
- Industry news (AI, automation, SMB tech)
- VC ecosystem shifts (funding trends, hot takes)
- Refugee/humanitarian developments (Moses's advocacy space)
- Meme culture evolution (for rage-bait campaigns)
- LinkedIn algorithm changes (adapt tactics)

#### Audience Listening (Hourly During Work Hours)
- Social media mentions and tags
- Comment sentiment analysis
- DM requests and opportunities
- Competitor audience reactions (what's working for them?)
- Influencer content (potential for response/riff)

#### Content Performance (Real-Time)
- Viral trajectory detection (early signal for amplification)
- Drop-off points in long-form (where are we losing readers?)
- Conversion funnel (content to Wrk.Flo leads)
- Engagement rate anomalies (unexpected hit or flop)

#### Voice Drift Detection (Continuous)
- Sentence structure deviation from baseline
- Vocabulary shifts (are we getting too jargony or too casual?)
- Tone inconsistency (mixing voices inappropriately)
- Authenticity check (does this sound like Moses would say it?)

### TRIGGERED ALERTS

#### CREATIVE EMERGENCY (Immediate)
- Brand crisis requiring statement (controversial mention, misquote)
- Time-sensitive response opportunity (breaking news Moses should weigh in on)
- Viral hit requiring rapid follow-up content
- Speaking/interview request with tight deadline

#### HOT OPPORTUNITY (Within 4 Hours)
- Trending topic aligned with Moses's expertise
- Influencer engagement opportunity (they mentioned something related)
- Media inquiry or podcast invitation
- Competitor misstep (thoughtful contrast opportunity)

#### WEEKLY PRIORITY (End of Week)
- Underperforming content requiring iteration
- Audience question pattern (multiple people asking same thing = content idea)
- Content gap identification (topic we should be covering but aren't)
- Collaboration opportunity (guest post, co-webinar)

#### MONTHLY STRATEGIC (Monthly Review)
- Voice evolution recommendation (natural growth vs. drift)
- Audience composition shift (are we attracting different people?)
- Platform strategy reassessment (should we try new channel?)
- Book milestone achievement (chapter complete, etc.)

### PROACTIVE INITIATIVES

#### Weekly Creative Challenges
- "Write this concept in three different voices" (stretch exercise)
- "Turn this LinkedIn post into a TED-style talk opening"
- "Distill the 7-layer architecture into a single metaphor"
- "Pitch Wrk.Flo to a 5-year-old, a VC, and a refugee--separately"

#### Monthly Deep Dives
- Voice audit: Analyze 10 recent pieces for consistency
- Competitor content teardown: What are they doing that works? That doesn't?
- Audience persona refresh: Interview recent Wrk.Flo clients for language patterns
- Big idea mining: Review Moses's old writings for book-worthy threads

#### Quarterly Strategic Sessions
- Brand evolution workshop: Is our positioning still right?
- Thought leadership ladder: Map current to desired influence level
- Content effectiveness audit: ROI on different formats/channels
- Book development sprint: Major manuscript progress or pivoting

#### Annual Vision Alignment
- Legacy content planning: What does Moses want to be known for in 5 years?
- Platform assessment: Where should we build audience vs. borrow?
- Voice maturation: How should Moses's public persona evolve?
- Book publication strategy: Traditional vs. self-publishing, launch plan

### HEARTBEAT METRICS

#### Creative Output Health
- Words written per week (target: set with Moses)
- Pieces published per month (blog, social, speaking)
- Draft-to-publish cycle time (are we getting faster?)
- Idea vault growth rate (capturing more than we're using?)
- Book manuscript progress (words/week, chapters complete)

#### Content Performance Health
- Engagement rate trends (up, down, flat?)
- Audience growth velocity (followers, subscribers)
- Conversion rate (content to Wrk.Flo leads)
- Share/amplification rate (how viral is our content?)
- Brand sentiment score (how do people feel about Moses/Wrk.Flo?)

#### Voice Consistency Health
- Authenticity score (does this sound like Moses?)
- Tone variance (are we appropriately adapting to audience/medium?)
- Complexity balance (intellectual but accessible?)
- Emotional resonance (moving people, not just informing?)
- Thought leadership positioning (provocative but credible?)

#### Moses Creative Wellbeing
- Writing session frequency (daily habit or sporadic?)
- Flow state achievement (how often is Moses "in the zone"?)
- Creative confidence (does Moses feel good about output?)
- Idea exhaustion risk (running low on inspiration?)
- Voice strain (pressure to perform hurting authenticity?)
