# MEMORY.md
## Memory Architecture

### 1. WORKING MEMORY (Session-Based)
**Retention**: Current session only

- Active piece being drafted
- Brainstorming session notes
- Real-time feedback and edits
- Flow state preservation
- Current creative direction

### 2. CREATIVE CACHE (7-Day Rolling)
**Retention**: Recent week's creative work

- Campaign concepts in development
- Draft iterations and versions
- A/B test variations
- Client feedback and revisions
- Meeting notes from creative sessions

### 3. CONTENT LIBRARY (Persistent)

#### Published Work Archive
- All published blog posts, articles, op-eds
- Social media posts (categorized by performance)
- Video scripts and final videos
- Email campaigns and newsletters
- Speaking presentations and transcripts
- Press releases and media coverage

#### Idea Vault
- Rough concepts and half-formed thoughts
- Moses's voice notes and fragments
- Overheard insights worth exploring
- "Someday/maybe" creative projects
- Rejected concepts worth revisiting

#### Source Material Library
- Moses's background documents (refugee story details)
- Wrk.Flo product documentation
- Client testimonials and quotes
- Competitive messaging analysis
- Industry research and reports
- Philosophical frameworks (quantum serendipity, 1% problems)

### 4. VOICE PROFILE (Persistent)

#### Moses's Natural Voice
- Sentence structure patterns
- Vocabulary preferences (technical, humanistic, hybrid)
- Metaphor tendencies (running, navigation, physics)
- Rhythm and cadence preferences
- Humor style (dry, understated)
- Emotional range and boundaries

#### Audience-Specific Adaptations
- **VC pitch voice** (confident, data-driven, visionary)
- **Humanitarian voice** (empathetic, urgent, hopeful)
- **Technical voice** (precise, architecture-focused)
- **Founder voice** (vulnerable, ambitious, authentic)
- **Refugee advocate voice** (fierce, personal, political)

### 5. CREATIVE PATTERNS

#### What Works Archive
- High-performing headlines and hooks
- Successful campaign frameworks
- Viral content anatomy
- Conversion-driving copy elements
- Speaking moments that landed
- Stories that moved people

#### What Doesn't Work Archive
- Failed campaigns and why
- Tone-deaf messaging attempts
- Overcomplicated concepts
- Audience mismatches
- Timing mistakes
- Off-brand deviations

### 6. REFERENCE LIBRARY

#### Stylistic Influences
- Writers Moses admires (identify and emulate strengths)
- Competitor messaging (what to avoid, what to learn from)
- Industry thought leaders (positioning analysis)
- Historical speeches and essays (rhetorical devices)
- Refugee narratives (authentic representation)

#### Fact Repository
- Wrk.Flo metrics and milestones (7 clients, $10K+ MRR, 0% churn)
- Moses's biographical facts (Augustana, mid-distance runner, gener8tor MD)
- Refugee statistics and context
- VC industry data (funding disparities, success rates)
- AI/automation market trends

## Memory Retrieval System

- **Semantic search**: "Find all content about quantum serendipity"
- **Tone matching**: "Show me pieces with fierce advocacy voice"
- **Performance ranking**: "What headlines drove most clicks?"
- **Audience filtering**: "Content for SMB owner persona only"
- **Chronological evolution**: "How has the Wrk.Flo pitch evolved?"

## Memory Maintenance

- **Daily**: Archive drafts, tag performance data
- **Weekly**: Synthesize what worked, update voice profile
- **Monthly**: Curate idea vault, refresh source material
- **Quarterly**: Major content audit, pattern analysis, strategic shifts
