# HEARTBEAT.md
## Proactive Monitoring & Engagement System

### DAILY RHYTHMS

#### Morning Briefing (6:00 AM)
- Weather and commute (if applicable)
- Calendar overview and prep needs
- Overnight developments (emails, news, Slack)
- Priority queue for the day (top 3 focuses)
- Energy optimization recommendations

#### Midday Check-in (12:00 PM)
- Morning progress review
- Urgent items requiring attention
- Afternoon focus recommendations
- Meeting prep for PM sessions
- Quick wins to maintain momentum

#### Evening Wrap-up (6:00 PM)
- Day accomplishment summary
- Tomorrow's prep and blockers
- Open loops requiring closure
- Personal time recommendations
- Gratitude/win celebration

#### Night Watch (10:00 PM)
- Sleep hygiene reminder
- Next-day preview
- Emergency monitoring only
- Overnight processing queue

### WEEKLY RHYTHMS

#### Monday: STRATEGIC PLANNING
- Week ahead overview
- Goal alignment check (OKRs, milestones)
- Resource allocation review
- Risk identification

#### Wednesday: PROGRESS PULSE
- Mid-week status on key initiatives
- Pivot recommendations if needed
- Stakeholder update prep
- Team sync notes

#### Friday: RETROSPECTIVE & PREVIEW
- Week wins and lessons
- Next week setup
- Investor update draft (if needed)
- Personal reflection prompts

#### Sunday: PREPARATION
- Upcoming week deep dive
- Calendar optimization
- Research packet for Monday
- Mental/physical prep recommendations

### CONTINUOUS MONITORING

#### Email Triage (Every 30 min during work hours)
- Urgent/important flagging
- Auto-draft responses for review
- Meeting request evaluation
- Follow-up tracking

#### Client Health Scores (Real-time)
- Usage pattern anomalies
- Support ticket sentiment
- Payment status
- Churn risk signals

#### Fundraising Pipeline (Daily)
- Investor interaction tracking
- Next action deadlines
- Warm intro opportunities
- Competitive funding announcements

#### Competitive Intelligence (Daily)
- Product launches in AI/automation space
- Pricing changes
- Key hires at competitors
- Industry analyst reports

#### Personal Wellbeing (Continuous)
- Calendar density (meeting overload detection)
- Communication tone analysis (stress indicators)
- Sleep/health data (if available)
- Time since last break/exercise

### TRIGGERED ALERTS

#### DEFCON 1 (Immediate notification)
- Client churn signal
- Critical system failure
- Investor deadline <24hrs
- Legal/compliance issue
- Personal emergency

#### DEFCON 2 (Within 2 hours)
- Client escalation
- Fundraising opportunity
- Competitive threat
- Key hire opportunity
- Media opportunity

#### DEFCON 3 (End of day)
- Meeting prep needed
- Follow-up overdue
- Goal trajectory off-track
- Interesting pattern detected
- Learning opportunity

#### DEFCON 4 (Weekly summary)
- Trend analysis
- Low-priority opportunities
- Nice-to-know updates
- Long-term considerations

### PROACTIVE INITIATIVES

#### Monthly Deep Dives
- Financial health comprehensive review
- Client portfolio optimization analysis
- Product roadmap realignment
- Personal goal progress assessment
- Relationship health check (professional/personal)

#### Quarterly Strategic Sessions
- Market position reevaluation
- Fundraising strategy update
- Team/org design planning
- Personal development planning
- Major pivot considerations

#### Annual Vision Alignment
- Mission/values check
- Big bet evaluation
- Exit strategy considerations
- Personal life integration
- Legacy planning

### HEARTBEAT METRICS

#### System Health
- Response time (target: <5 sec for queries)
- Accuracy rate (verified predictions)
- Proactive value adds per week (target: >20)
- False positive alert rate (minimize)
- Memory retrieval accuracy

#### Moses Health
- Stress indicators (language, calendar density)
- Productivity patterns (energy curves)
- Work-life balance score
- Goal progression rate
- Satisfaction signals (explicit + inferred)

#### Business Health
- MRR trajectory
- Client satisfaction scores
- Pipeline velocity
- Burn rate vs. runway
- Team morale (as it grows)
